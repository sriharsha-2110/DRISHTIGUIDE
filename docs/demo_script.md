# DRISHTIGUIDE AI — Viva & Presentation Demo Script

Follow this step-by-step presentation flow for college project evaluation and faculty live demonstrations.

---

## Step 1: Introduction & Problem Statement (1 Minute)
> "Respected Evaluators, today we present **DRISHTIGUIDE AI — Let AI Be Your Eyes**. Traditional computer vision projects announce every object in sight, creating audio overload for visually impaired users. DRISHTIGUIDE AI solves this by introducing **Intelligent Guidance Prioritization**, deciding what the user actually needs to know right now."

---

## Step 2: Mobile Interface Demonstration (2 Minutes)
1. Open the **Mobile Application** view.
2. Highlight system diagnostic indicators:
   - Voice Guidance: **ON**
   - Camera: **CONNECTED**
   - AI System: **ONLINE**
   - Earphones: **CONNECTED**
3. Press **"LET'S START THE JOURNEY"**.
4. Demonstrate real-time camera feed analysis and short earphone audio announcements (e.g. *"Stairs ahead. Move carefully."*).

---

## Step 3: Laptop AI Research Dashboard Demonstration (3 Minutes)
1. Switch to the **Laptop Research Dashboard** view.
2. Show **Live Vision Tab**: Bounding boxes drawn in real-time, class confidence %, horizontal position (`CENTER`), approximate distance (`NEAR`), and risk level (`HIGH`).
3. Open **AI Explanation Panel ("WHY DID AI GIVE THIS WARNING?")**:
   - Trace step-by-step decision flow: Detection → Bounding Box Centroid → Movement Tracking → Risk Score Calculation → Priority Filter → Spoken Voice Instruction.
4. Show **Model Specs & Training Tabs**: Precision (89.2%), Recall (86.5%), mAP@50 (91.4%), FPS (~70 FPS on CPU).
5. Show **Confusion Matrix**: Demonstrate True Positives, False Positives, and False Negatives for navigation hazards.

---

## Step 4: Emergency Critical Override Demonstration (Highlight Event!)
1. Trigger **Scenario 4: Vehicle Approaching**.
2. Point out that the system immediately interrupts routine audio to alert:
   > **"STOP. Vehicle approaching."**
3. Show that duplicate announcements are suppressed to keep speech non-intrusive.

---

## Step 5: Offline Demo Mode (Fail-Safe)
1. Demonstrate that if camera permissions or internet connections drop, **Demo Mode** allows selecting pre-configured scenarios or uploading custom test images/videos for full evaluation.
